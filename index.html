From 5 Hectares to 500+ Hectares

Founded in 2018 in Banyuwangi, East Java, Avocado Indonesia began with a simple vision: to introduce premium Indonesian avocados to international markets.

What started as a 5-hectare family farm has now expanded into over 500 hectares of avocado plantations, working together with more than 200 local farmers across East Java.

Today, we proudly supply retail chains, distributors, and HoReCa businesses in Singapore, Malaysia, and the Middle East.

Mission

Deliver the healthiest premium avocados while empowering local farming communities sustainably.

Vision

To become Southeast Asia’s most trusted premium avocado supplier by 2030.

Our Plantation

Located in East Java’s volcanic highlands, ideal for year-round avocado cultivation.

Sorting Facility

Every avocado is carefully sorted, graded, and packed to export standards.

Sustainability

Zero-waste farming, drip irrigation, and solar-powered warehouse systems.
